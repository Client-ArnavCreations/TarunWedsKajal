Women: pastel sarees or suits, light lehengas, chikankari or thread work, floral prints, elegant traditional jewellery in pink, peach, mint, lavender or powder blue.
Men: pastel kurta with Nehru jacket, light kurta pajama, pastel indo-western, subtle prints or florals, stole or pocket square.

Sangeet · Shimmers & Glams

Women: indo-western gowns, glam lehengas, shimmery sarees, sequins and metallics.

Women: yellow sarees, suits or lehengas, floral or mirror work, light breezy fabrics, flower jewellery.

Shades: lemon, pastel, mustard, marigold, golden yellow.

Men: sherwani or bandhgala, indo-western achkan, safa, stoles and royal accessories.
